# ByeByeMoneyList

## Your spending, organized automatically.

**ByeByeMoneyList is an AI-powered shopping and expense tracking app that turns receipts into organized financial data — in seconds.**

Take a photo of your receipt. AI recognizes the purchased items, identifies the store, categorizes your purchases according to your personal category structure, and records everything automatically.

**One click to scan. One click to save. Your expenses are organized.**

### Stop typing. Start tracking.

Keeping track of everyday spending shouldn't mean manually entering every purchase.

With ByeByeMoneyList, simply scan your receipt and let AI do the work.

**Scan → Recognize → Categorize → Save.**

The app can automatically identify:

* 🧾 Purchased products and their prices
* 🏪 Stores and merchants
* 📂 Expense categories
* 💰 Individual expenses

Your category structure is yours to control. Start with the predefined hierarchy, then modify, reorganize, or expand it to match the way you manage your money.

### Understand where your money goes

Once your expenses are recorded, ByeByeMoneyList turns your data into clear statistics and visual reports.

Track your spending by:

* **Category**
* **Store**
* **Month**
* **Year**

See your spending patterns through detailed statistics and charts, and get a clearer picture of where your money is going.

### More than expense tracking

ByeByeMoneyList brings everyday financial organization together in one place.

**Shopping lists**
Create and manage the things you need to buy.

**Income**
Record your income manually and keep it alongside your expenses.

**Subscriptions**
Keep track of recurring subscriptions and payments.

**Recurring purchases**
Record regular expenses so they don't disappear from your overview.

### Everything at a glance

The **Dashboard** gives you a quick overview of your financial activity.

See your recent spending, income, categories, stores, and other key information without digging through individual transactions.

## Your money. Your categories. Your data.

Everyone manages money differently.

That's why ByeByeMoneyList doesn't force you into a rigid budgeting system. Start with the built-in category structure and customize it to fit your own life.

Create the hierarchy that makes sense to you — and let AI organize your purchases around it.

## Make expense tracking effortless.

Forget spreadsheets. Forget manually entering every item from every receipt.

**Take a photo. Let AI handle the rest.**

### ByeByeMoneyList

**The simple way to turn everyday purchases into organized financial insights.**

### My recommendation: use a light landing page

Even though your app has a dark theme, I **wouldn't automatically make the entire website dark**.

Your logo is extremely bright and playful. A predominantly white/light-blue website would give it room to breathe and make the product feel:

**simple + approachable + modern + energetic**

rather than overly "serious fintech."

I'd use dark sections strategically.

# 6. I would use orange sparingly

Your orange is actually quite distinctive because it comes from the mascot.

But I **wouldn't make orange another primary UI color**.

Otherwise you'll end up with:

🔵 blue
🟢 green
🟠 orange
🌑 navy

all competing for attention.

Instead, reserve orange for:

* mascot illustrations
* occasional decorative elements
* shopping-related visual elements
* tiny highlights
* maybe a special notification/state

Think of orange as the **personality color**, rather than the functional color.

## One thing I'd change from the current logo

I actually like the logo's energy. **Don't make the landing page as cartoonish as the logo.**

That's the trap I'd avoid.

The mascot can provide personality, while the website UI should be much cleaner:

**Playful logo + sophisticated interface = memorable brand.**

If everything is bright, rounded, cartoonish and colorful, the product may feel less like a serious expense tracker.

I'd aim for something closer to:

> **Duolingo's visual confidence + modern fintech cleanliness + AI-product simplicity**

without copying any of those brands.

And because you have Android screenshots, I would make the **receipt → AI recognition → organized expenses** workflow the centerpiece of the page rather than spending too much space explaining generic features like "track income" or "view charts." That's the part of ByeByeMoneyList that immediately communicates *why this app exists*.
